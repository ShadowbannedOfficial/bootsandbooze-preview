Reservations

Just walk in.

No booking required, no booking fee, no waiting for an email that says a human will confirm. The courtyard is open-air with room to spread out, and getting in does not require a booking.

The honest version

How it actually works

Walk up to 3014 Main, come through the gate, and find a seat. That’s it. There is no host stand and nobody is going to check a list.

Seating is first-come. Picnic tables and benches on the turf, some under the pergola, some out in the open. The bar is in the courtyard and the kitchen window is on the side — order there, take a number, and eat wherever you landed.

If you want a table with a good angle on the screens for a Cowboys game, come early. If you’re rolling in at 10 PM on a Saturday, expect to stand for a bit before something opens up. Both are fine.

Good to know

What to expect

Nothing on the floor is held. Walk-ins are seated first-come and a table is only yours once you’re at it. If a particular spot matters to you — the long bench, the table under the pergola — come take it early.

The courtyard is open-air. Part of it is covered, most of it isn’t. Weather is the one thing we can’t book around. On a rain night, check Instagram before you drive.

Game days bring screens, not seating. We put the sound on for Cowboys games in and out. We don’t hold tables for kickoff, so a group that wants one should arrive together.

Hours shift on holidays. We post changes to Instagram before we change them here. If you’re driving in for something specific, it’s worth a look.

Quick answers

The questions
we get most

Do I need a reservation?

No. Walk in. Seating is first-come and there’s no booking fee on walk-in tables. For groups of eight or more, send us a request above so we can be ready.

Is there a cover charge?

Never. Not on game days, not on the weekend, not for the band.

Can we get married today?

No — Texas requires 72 hours between the license and the ceremony. Get the license at the county clerk at least three days ahead, then book the chapel. Full details on the chapel page.

Is the courtyard dog-friendly?

Yes, on a leash. Ask the bar for a water bowl.

Where do we park?

Street parking on Main and the surrounding blocks, plus the paid lots and garages in Deep Ellum — the nearest are on Commerce and Elm. It’s a walkable district, so a lot four blocks away is normal on a Saturday. Rideshare drops at the corner of Main and Crowdus.
