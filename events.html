What the week
looks like

No cover, ever. Programming rotates — here’s the shape of it, and the day-of lives on Instagram.

The courtyard

Live music on the stage when the weather cooperates, a DJ between sets, and the big screens carrying whatever matters that night. Dogs welcome on the turf — water bowls are behind the bar, just ask.

Seating is first-come. Benches and picnic tables, some under the pergola, some out in the open. Nothing on the floor can be held, so if you want one particular table, come and take it.

The window

Open when the bar is open. Order up, take a number, and eat wherever you land — no host stand, no table service, no check to chase at the end of the night.
