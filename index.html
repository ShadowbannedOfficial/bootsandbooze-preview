The idea

A courtyard, a kitchen window, and a chapel. No cover. No dress code. No rush.

Boots & Booze is built for the long part of the night. Sit down in the courtyard with a frozen drink and stay for the second half. Order from the window when you get hungry. Or walk to the back and get married — we have a room for that.

High-end meets high-energy, without the velvet rope. The drinks are strong, the music is live, and the good times never feel rushed. Pull up a bench. The room is yours for as long as you want it.
