The Courtyard

An open-air room covered in turf, ringed by a shipping-container building and a painted brick wall. Picnic tables and benches under string lights and under the pergola. The bar is here, so is the stage, and so is the wall of screens for whatever game is on.

It’s dog-friendly. It’s first-come. Part of it is covered and the rest is Dallas sky. When the room is loud it’s built for it, and when it’s empty you can hear the string lights hum.

The Window

A walk-up kitchen on the side of the courtyard. Southern comfort food with a creative twist, served on a tray with a number. There’s no host stand and no table service for food — you order, you take a number, you find a bench.

The full menu is still being published. Ask at the counter for what’s on, and tell them about allergies before you order.

Where do we park?

Street parking on Main and the surrounding blocks, plus the paid lots and garages throughout Deep Ellum. The nearest are off Commerce and Elm. On a Saturday night expect to walk a few blocks — that’s just the district. Rideshare drop-off at Main and Crowdus is easiest.

Is it dog friendly?

Yes. The courtyard is dog-friendly, on a leash. Ask the bar for a water bowl — we keep them behind the counter.

Do you take reservations?

Walk-in for everything under eight people. First-come seating, no booking fee, no waiting on a confirmation email. For eight or more, send a group request and we’ll reply the same day.

Can we get married today?

No. Texas law requires 72 hours between the marriage license being issued and the ceremony. Get the license from the county clerk at least three days before your date, then book the chapel. The license is good for 90 days.

Is there a cover charge?

No cover, ever. No dress code either. Boots are encouraged but not required.

Is there a dress code?

No. Boots are encouraged, not required. The courtyard is turf and picnic tables — dress for the weather and you’ll be right.

When does the kitchen open and close?

The window is open when the bar is open. The full food menu is still posting — ask at the counter for what’s on that day.

What’s opening next?

An attached indoor dining room and lounge — phase two, opening soon. We’ll announce it on Instagram and in the email list before anywhere else.
